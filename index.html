Quiet games, one touch.

We make small games you can play with a single press and put down again without guilt. Soft failure, gentle sound, and targets instead of rankings.

Our first game

Long Shadow

Hold to bring the sun low. The lower it sinks, the longer your shadow grows, and a long shadow is solid ground. Bridge the chasms, cool the scorched earth, and lift the sun again when the briars wake.

Walk 20 handmade levels, run a full day from dawn to dusk with three lives, take on the daily run, or wander in Zen with no clock and no failing. Endless opens once you have gathered enough fireflies and flowers, and a shop holds new characters, hats and a cherry-blossom Tokyo Spring.

  • One hold
  • 20 levels
  • No ads
  • Plays offline
Coming to Google Play

Our second game

WanderfoldPaper Runner

Hold to fold the world. Let go to flatten it. A paper scout runs on her own across folded mountains, ink rivers and sleepy valleys, and you decide when to hold and when to let go so the gaps close and the creases stay low.

Play 20 handmade levels, an endless run, a new daily seed, calm Zen, or the Gauntlet with every obstacle at once. Gather sparks and lanterns for scouts, scarves, hats, trails, papers and whole new worlds.

  • One hold
  • 20 levels
  • No ads
  • Plays offline
Coming to Google Play

Our third game

Coreleap

Tap to dive through the core. A small light runs the rim of a tiny planet on its own, and you decide when to leap straight through the middle and come out on the far side. Dive at the last moment for a perfect, and chain them into a combo.

Run endless at three paces, through four changing biomes, with ten hazards that arrive one at a time. Take on the daily run, earn Bronze, Silver and Gold medals, fill the Codex, and unlock seven worlds with the stardust you gather.

  • One tap
  • Endless
  • No ads
  • Plays offline
Coming to Google Play

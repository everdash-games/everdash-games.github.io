Privacy policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This policy covers the Android game Long Shadow · Everdash and this website. It is written to be plain: our games are built to work without collecting anything about you.

The game

Long Shadow does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. Specifically:

  • There is no account, sign-in or sign-up.
  • There are no ads and no advertising identifiers.
  • There are no analytics or tracking tools.
  • There are no real-money purchases. Items in the game are earned by playing.
  • There are no leaderboards or online features, and the game works without an internet connection.

What stays on your device

Your progress, best scores, settings (such as sound level) and the items you collect are saved only in the game's storage on your own device. We cannot see them. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, they are removed.

Sharing your result

The game can make a postcard image of your run and a short line of text you can copy. These are created on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to save the image or paste the text into another app yourself, and what that app does with it is covered by its own policy.

Permissions

The game does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not run analytics or set cookies on it. GitHub, as the host, may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. If a future update to a game collects any data, this policy will say so before that update is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy: everdash.games@gmail.com.

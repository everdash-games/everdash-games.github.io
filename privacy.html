Privacy policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy covers the Android games Long Shadow, Wanderfold: Paper Runner and Coreleap: Dive Runner, all made by Everdash, and this website. It is written to be plain: our games are built to work without collecting anything about you.

The games

Our games do not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. Specifically:

  • There is no account, sign-in or sign-up.
  • There are no ads and no advertising identifiers.
  • There are no analytics or tracking tools.
  • There are no real-money purchases. Items in the games are earned by playing.
  • There are no leaderboards or online features, and the games work without an internet connection. They do not send or receive any data of their own.

What stays on your device

Your progress, best scores, settings (such as sound level and accessibility options), daily streaks and the items you collect are saved only in the game's storage on your own device. We cannot see them. If you uninstall a game or clear its data, they are removed.

Coreleap can also make a backup code, a line of text that holds your progress. It is created on your device and is only copied to your clipboard if you tap Copy. We never receive it. If you paste a code back in, it is read on your device only.

If you have Android's own backup turned on, Android may include a game's saved data in your personal Google backup. That is controlled by you and Google, and we do not receive it.

Sharing your result

A game can make a postcard image of your run and a short line of text you can copy. These are created on your device. If you tap Share, the image is saved briefly in the game's own temporary cache folder on your device so your phone's share sheet can pass it to the app you choose. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you pick an app yourself, and what that app does with it is covered by its own policy. Tapping Copy only places the text on your clipboard.

Vibration

Coreleap can vibrate your phone for feedback. This uses your device's vibration only, collects nothing, and can be turned off in Settings.

Links

The "More from Everdash" button in each game opens this website in your browser, and Coreleap's Settings also has a "Privacy policy" link that opens this page. This is the only time the games reach the internet, and it happens only when you tap it.

Permissions

The games do not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files. They use the internet permission only for the links above, and Coreleap also uses the vibration permission.

Children

Because the games collect no personal information, they collect none from children either.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not run analytics or set cookies on it. GitHub, as the host, may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. If a future update to a game collects any data, this policy will say so before that update is released.
